SITE CONDITIONS
i) The structure shall be designed for the exposure condition severe as defined in IS 456:2000.

4 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — CONCRETE AND REINFORCEMENT
i) All plain and reinforced concrete work shall conform to IS 456:2000.
ii) All RCC work shall be of grade M30.
iii) Nominal cover to reinforcement shall be 30mm.
iv) The free water-cement ratio shall not exceed 0.45.
v) Minimum cement content shall be 320 kg/m3.
vi) Cement shall be ordinary Portland cement 43 grade conforming to IS 269:2015.
vii) Coarse and fine aggregates shall conform to IS 383:2016.
viii) Reinforcement shall be Fe 500D TMT bars conforming to IS 1786:2008.
ix) Concrete shall be design mix proportioned as per IS 10262:2019.
x) Curing shall be continued for a minimum period of 10 days.
xi) Concrete shall be compacted with mechanical needle vibrators.

SECTION 5: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
i) Cube tests for compressive strength shall be carried out at 7 and 28 days.
ii) Manufacturer's test certificates shall be furnished for each lot of cement and reinforcement steel.
iii) The equipment shall be inspected by the Engineer-in-charge at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.

6 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
i) The contractor shall submit detailed shop drawings for approval within 21 days of award of work.
ii) Three sets of operation and maintenance manuals shall be handed over on completion. The complete installation shall be guaranteed against defective design, material and workmanship for a period of 12 months from the date of handing over.
iii) The contractor shall train the Department's operating staff in operation and routine maintenance.
