SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers supply, installation, testing and commissioning of lt electrical distribution system complete in all respects.

SITE CONDITIONS
i) All equipment shall be suitable for operation at an ambient air temperature not exceeding 40 Deg.C. The equipment shall be suitable for relative humidity up to 95 per cent.
ii) Site altitude does not exceed 1000 m above MSL.

SECTION 4: MAIN LT PANEL
i) Switchboards shall be designed, manufactured and tested in accordance with IS/IEC 61439-1:2020 and IS/IEC 61439-2:2020.
ii) Panels shall be free standing, totally enclosed, made of 2 mm. thick CRCA sheet with powder coated finish.
iii) The panel shall be suitable for 415 Volts ± 10 per cent, 50 Hz, 3 phase 4 wire system.
iv) The horizontal busbar shall be high conductivity aluminium alloy of 1600A rating.
v) Short time withstand rating of the switchboard shall be 50kA for 1 s.
vi) Enclosure protection class for the switchboard shall be IP 54.
vii) Internal separation of functional units shall be Form 4.
viii) Incoming breaker shall be 1600A four pole draw-out ACB as per IS/IEC 60947-2:2003 with microprocessor release.
ix) All outgoing MCCBs shall have a rated service breaking capacity of not less than 36kA.
x) Each incomer shall be provided with a digital multifunction meter with RS-485 port.

5 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
i) LT power cables shall be XLPE insulated 1.1 kV grade armoured cables with aluminium conductors as per IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
ii) Size of the incoming feeder cable shall be 3.5 core x 300 sqmm.
iii) Continuous conductor operating temperature of XLPE cables shall be 90 Deg.C. Copper control cables of 2.5 sqmm shall be PVC insulated heavy duty type as per IS 1554 (Part 1).
iv) Cable conductors shall meet the resistance requirements of IS 8130 for class 2 conductors.
v) Cables shall be laid directly in ground at a depth of 750 mm. with brick protection.
vi) Terminations shall be made with brass double compression glands and tinned copper lugs. Cable drums shall carry marking of size, type, length and manufacturer's name.

6 TESTS AND INSPECTION
i) Each switchboard shall be subjected to routine verification tests as per IS/IEC 61439-1 at the works.
ii) The bidder shall furnish type test reports of the switchboard and breakers from an NABL accredited laboratory.
iii) Acceptance tests on cables shall be carried out as per IS 10810 in presence of the Engineer.
iv) Manufacturer's test certificates shall accompany every cable drum supplied. Pre-dispatch inspection shall be offered to the Department's authorised representative with 15 days notice.

7 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
i) Shop drawings and schematic diagrams shall be got approved from the Engineer-in-charge before manufacture.
ii) O&M manuals, as-built drawings and catalogues shall be furnished in triplicate at the time of handing over.
iii) The supplier shall replace free of cost any part found defective within the defect liability period of 24 months.
iv) Training of two operators for one week shall be imparted by the supplier after commissioning.
