SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers hostel block construction — civil and internal electrical works complete in all respects.

3 SITE CONDITIONS
3.1 Exposure condition shall be considered as severe for all members.

SECTION 4: CONCRETE AND REINFORCEMENT
4.1 Design and construction of RCC shall be in accordance with IS 456:2000.
4.2 Grade of concrete for all structural members shall be M 30.
4.3 Clear cover to main reinforcement shall be 45mm for all members.
4.4 Maximum free water cement ratio shall be 0.45.
4.5 Cement content shall not be less than 320 Kg/cum of concrete.
4.6 OPC 43 grade cement as per IS 269:2015 shall be used.
4.7 Aggregates shall comply with the requirements of IS 383:2016.
4.8 High strength deformed bars of grade Fe 500D as per IS 1786:2008 shall be used.
4.9 Mix design shall be carried out in accordance with IS 10262:2019.
4.10 Concrete shall be kept continuously moist for at least 10 days.
4.11 Compaction shall be done by immersion vibrators.

5 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — INTERNAL WIRING AND EARTHING
5.1 The internal wiring installation shall comply with IS 732:2019.
5.2 Earthing arrangement of the installation shall be TN-S system.
5.3 All wiring shall be drawn through heavy gauge PVC conduits recessed in walls.
5.4 Building wires shall be 450/750 V grade PVC insulated multistrand copper conductor as per IS 694:2010.
5.5 Minimum conductor size shall be 1.5 sq.mm for lighting points and 4 sq.mm for power points.
5.6 MCBs shall be C-curve, as per IS/IEC 60898-1, with rated short circuit capacity of 10 k.A..
5.7 Final circuit MCBs shall be rated between 6 A and 32 A.
5.8 Socket outlet circuits shall be protected by 30 mA RCCBs conforming to IS 12640 (Part 1).
5.9 The earthing system shall be designed and installed as per IS 3043:1987.
5.10 Main distribution board shall be earthed through two separate pipe earth electrodes.
5.11 Combined resistance of the earthing system shall be less than 1 Ohm.

6 TESTS AND INSPECTION
6.1 Cube tests for compressive strength shall be done in accordance with IS 516 (Part 1/Sec 1):2021.
6.2 Mill test certificates for steel and test certificates for cement shall be submitted for every consignment.
6.3 Samples of wires shall be tested as per IS 10810 at the manufacturer's works.
6.4 Tests shall be carried out on completion of the installation as per IS 732 and results recorded.
6.5 Pre-dispatch inspection shall be offered to the Department's authorised representative with 15 days notice.

7 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
7.1 Shop drawings and schematic diagrams shall be got approved from the Engineer-in-charge before manufacture.
7.2 O&M manuals, as-built drawings and catalogues shall be furnished in triplicate at the time of handing over.
7.3 The supplier shall replace free of cost any part found defective within the defect liability period of 24 months.
7.4 Training of two operators for one week shall be imparted by the supplier after commissioning.
