MADHYANCHAL VIDYUT VITRAN NIGAM LIMITED
Materials Management Directorate
Enquiry No. MVVNL/MM/DT-630/2026-27/41
Subject: Procurement of 630 kVA, 11/0.433 kV distribution transformers, 11 kV cables and LT circuit breakers under the RDSS scheme.

SECTION I — INVITATION FOR BIDS
1. Sealed bids are invited from manufacturers having valid BIS licence for distribution transformers.
2. The bidder shall have supplied at least 500 transformers of 630 kVA or higher rating to any State utility during the last five years.
3. Bids received after the due date will not be considered.

SECTION II — TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION FOR DISTRIBUTION TRANSFORMERS
2.1 Standard: The transformers shall conform to IS 1180 (Part 1):2014 with latest amendments.
2.2 Type: Outdoor, three phase, oil immersed, naturally cooled (ONAN), copper wound.
2.3 Rating: 630 kVA, 11/0.433 kV, Dyn11 vector group.
2.4 The transformers shall meet energy efficiency level 2 as specified in IS 1180 (Part 1).
2.5 Maximum total losses at 50 % and 100 % loading shall not exceed the values specified in the standard for the offered efficiency level.
2.6 Temperature rise of top oil shall not exceed 35 °C and of winding 40 °C over an ambient of 50 °C.
2.7 Off-circuit tap changer with taps from +2.5 % to -5 % in steps of 2.5 % shall be provided on the HV side.
2.8 The tank shall be of welded mild steel construction and shall be painted with epoxy based paint.
2.9 Each transformer shall be provided with a rating and diagram plate of anodised aluminium.
2.10 The no-load losses shall be as low as possible.

GUARANTEED TECHNICAL PARTICULARS — DISTRIBUTION TRANSFORMER
Rated power: 630 kVA
Rated HV voltage: 11 kV
Rated LV voltage: 433 V
Frequency: 50 Hz
Cooling: ONAN
Energy efficiency level: Level 2

SECTION III — TESTS
3.1 All routine tests shall be carried out on each transformer as per IS 2026 (Part 1):1977.
3.2 Type test reports of the offered design from a NABL accredited laboratory, not older than five years, shall be submitted with the bid.
3.3 The purchaser reserves the right to witness the tests at the manufacturer's works.

SECTION IV — 11 kV POWER CABLES
4.1 11 kV (E) grade, 3 core, 240 sq mm aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated, armoured cables conforming to IS 7098 (Part 1) shall be supplied.
4.2 Cables shall be supplied in non-returnable wooden drums of 250 m length.
4.3 Each drum shall be marked with the manufacturer's name, size, voltage grade and year of manufacture.

SECTION V — LT CIRCUIT BREAKERS
5.1 LT air circuit breakers of 1000 A rating shall conform to IS 13947 (Part 2):1993.
5.2 The rated service short circuit breaking capacity shall be 50 kA.
5.3 Breakers shall be provided with thermal-magnetic release and shunt trip.

SECTION VI — DELIVERY AND GUARANTEE
6.1 Delivery shall be completed within 120 days from the date of purchase order.
6.2 The transformers shall be guaranteed for 60 months from the date of commissioning.

Annexure A: Consignee-wise distribution of quantities (to be issued with the purchase order).
