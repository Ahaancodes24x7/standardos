SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers procurement of distribution transformers and lt cables complete in all respects.

PART C — DISTRIBUTION TRANSFORMERS
(a) Distribution transformers shall be designed and manufactured to IS 1180 (Part 1):2014.
(b) Transformer rating shall be 630 K.V.A., 11 kV / 433 V, Dyn11.
(c) Transformers shall be outdoor type, mineral oil filled, naturally cooled, with copper windings.
(d) Winding temperature rise shall be limited to 40 °C over a maximum ambient of 50 °C.
(e) Transformer tank shall be fabricated from mild steel plates and epoxy painted.

SECTION 4: LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
(a) LT power cables shall be XLPE insulated 1.1 kV grade armoured cables with aluminium conductors as per IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
(b) Continuous conductor operating temperature of XLPE cables shall be 90 °C.
(c) Copper control cables of 2.5 sq mm shall be PVC insulated heavy duty type as per IS 1554 (Part 1).
(d) Cable conductors shall meet the resistance requirements of IS 8130 for class 2 conductors.
(e) Cables shall be laid directly in ground at a depth of 750 mm. with brick protection.
(f) Terminations shall be made with brass double compression glands and tinned copper lugs.
(g) Cable drums shall carry marking of size, type, length and manufacturer's name.

SECTION 5: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
(a) Each transformer shall be subjected to routine tests in accordance with IS 2026 (Part 1):2011.
(b) The bidder shall submit type test certificates from an NABL accredited laboratory for the offered rating.
(c) Acceptance tests on cables shall be carried out as per IS 10810 in presence of the Engineer.
(d) Pre-dispatch inspection shall be offered to the Department's authorised representative with 15 days notice.

6 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING

(b) O&M manuals, as-built drawings and catalogues shall be furnished in triplicate at the time of handing over.
