SECTION 1 — INSTRUCTIONS TO BIDDERS
1.1 The bidder shall submit the EMD in the form of an online payment or bank guarantee from a scheduled bank.
1.2 The bid shall remain valid for 90 days from the date of opening of the technical bid.
1.3 Bidders shall have completed at least one similar work of value not less than Rs. 1.47 crore during the last seven years.
1.4 Conditional bids will be summarily rejected.

SECTION 2 — SCOPE OF WORK
2.1 This specification covers design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning of the main LT panel, sub-distribution boards, power cables, earthing and associated works.
2.2 The work shall be executed in accordance with CPWD General Specifications for Electrical Works (Part I Internal and Part II External), as amended up to date.

SECTION 3 — SITE CONDITIONS
3.1 The equipment shall be designed for a maximum ambient temperature of 50 °C and relative humidity up to 95 %.
3.2 The site is located at an altitude of less than 1000 m above mean sea level.
3.3 The area falls under seismic zone III.

SECTION 4 — MAIN LT PANEL
4.1 The main LT panel shall be of cubicle type, floor mounted, totally enclosed and fabricated from CRCA sheet steel of 2 mm thickness.
4.2 The panel shall conform to IS/IEC 61439-1:2020 and IS/IEC 61439-2:2020.
4.3 The panel shall have Form 3b internal separation.
4.4 The degree of protection of the panel enclosure shall be IP54.
4.5 The main busbars shall be of electrolytic grade aluminium alloy and shall be rated for 2500 A continuous current.
4.6 The busbars and the panel shall withstand a short circuit current of 50 kA for 1 second.
4.7 Incomers shall be 2500 A, 4 pole, electrically operated air circuit breakers conforming to IS/IEC 60947-2:2003 with microprocessor based release.
4.8 Outgoing feeders shall be provided with MCCBs of 50 kA breaking capacity conforming to IS/IEC 60947-2.
4.9 Metering shall include multifunction meters of accuracy class 0.5 with RS-485 communication port.

GUARANTEED TECHNICAL PARTICULARS — MAIN LT PANEL (to be filled by the bidder)
Rated operational voltage: 415 V, 3 phase, 4 wire
Rated frequency: 50 Hz
Design ambient temperature: 45 °C
Rated current of main busbars: 2500 A
Rated short-time withstand current: 50 kA for 1 s
Degree of protection: IP54
Form of separation: Form 3b

SECTION 5 — SUB-DISTRIBUTION BOARDS AND PROTECTIVE DEVICES
5.1 Sub-distribution boards shall be double door, dust and vermin proof, with IP43 degree of protection.
5.2 Miniature circuit breakers shall conform to IS/IEC 60898-1 and shall have a breaking capacity of 10 kA.
5.3 RCCBs with rated residual operating current of 30 mA shall be provided for all socket outlet circuits and shall conform to IS 12640 (Part 1).
5.4 All protective devices shall be of the same make in a distribution board.

SECTION 6 — POWER CABLES
6.1 Power cables shall be 1.1 kV grade, 3.5 core, aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated, armoured and PVC sheathed, conforming to IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
6.2 The main incoming cable shall be of 300 sq mm size and run in two parallel runs.
6.3 Cables shall be laid in RCC trenches or on perforated cable trays as shown in the drawings.
6.4 Cable ends shall be terminated using double compression glands and crimped aluminium lugs.
6.5 All cables shall be tested for insulation resistance before and after laying.

SECTION 7 — EARTHING
7.1 Earthing shall be carried out in accordance with IS 3043:1987.
7.2 The system shall be TN-S earthing arrangement.
7.3 Four numbers of pipe earth electrodes shall be provided for the LT panel.
7.4 The combined earth resistance shall not exceed 1 ohm.
7.5 Two separate and distinct earth connections shall be provided for each panel and distribution board.

SECTION 8 — TESTING, INSPECTION AND DOCUMENTATION
8.1 The LT panel shall be inspected at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.
8.2 Type test certificates of the panel from a NABL accredited laboratory shall be submitted along with the technical bid.
8.3 Routine verification of the assemblies shall be carried out as per IS/IEC 61439-1.
8.4 The contractor shall submit shop drawings for approval within 21 days of award of work.
8.5 Three sets of as-built drawings and operation and maintenance manuals shall be handed over on completion.
8.6 The complete installation shall be guaranteed against defects for a period of 12 months from the date of handing over.
