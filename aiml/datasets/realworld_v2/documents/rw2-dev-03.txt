3 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — HORIZONTAL CENTRIFUGAL PUMPS
1. Pumps shall conform to IS 1520:1980.
2. Each pump shall deliver 250 m³/h against a total head of 45 m. The pump efficiency at the duty point shall not be less than 78%.
3. Casing shall be of cast iron and impeller shall be of bronze.
4. Noise level at 1 m from the pumpset shall not exceed 85 dB(A).

PUMP DATA SHEET
Discharge: 250 m³/h
Total head: 45 m
Make and type: (to be furnished by the bidder)

4 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — DRIVE MOTORS
1. Motors shall be squirrel cage induction motors conforming to IS 325:1996.
2. The rated output of the motor shall be 37kW.
3. Motors shall be suitable for 415V ± 15%, 50Hz ± 5%, 3 phase supply.
4. Motors shall be energy efficient.
5. Motors shall have class F insulation with temperature rise limited to class B and shall be suitable for S1 duty.
6. Synchronous speed of the motor shall be 1500 RPM.

5 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
1. Power cables shall be 11 kV grade, aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated, armoured and PVC sheathed, conforming to IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
2. The main feeder cable shall be 3.5 core 300 sq.mm.
3. The cables shall be suitable for a maximum continuous conductor temperature of 90 deg C. Control cables shall be PVC insulated, copper conductor of 2.5 sq.mm, 1.1 kV grade, conforming to IS 1554 (Part 1).
4. Conductors shall be stranded, class 2, conforming to IS 8130. Cables shall be laid in RCC trenches or on perforated cable trays as shown in the drawings.
5. Cable ends shall be terminated using double compression glands and crimped lugs.
6. Each drum shall be marked with the manufacturer's name, size, voltage grade and year of manufacture.

SECTION 6: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
1. Pumps shall be tested at the works in accordance with IS 9137. Pumps shall bear the ISI mark.
2. Routine tests on motors shall be carried out as per IS 4029.
3. Motors shall bear the ISI mark.
4. Cables shall be tested in accordance with IS 10810.
5. Routine test certificates of cables shall be furnished with each consignment. The equipment shall be inspected by the Engineer-in-charge at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.

7 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
1. The contractor shall submit detailed shop drawings for approval within 21 days of award of work.
2. Three sets of operation and maintenance manuals shall be handed over on completion.
3. The complete installation shall be guaranteed against defective design, material and workmanship for a period of 12 months from the date of handing over.
4. The contractor shall train the Department's operating staff in operation and routine maintenance.
