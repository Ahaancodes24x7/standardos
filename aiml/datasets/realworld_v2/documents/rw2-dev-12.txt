SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers internal electrification including wiring, distribution boards and earthing complete in all respects.

PART C — INTERNAL WIRING AND EARTHING
(a) Wiring shall be carried out as per IS 732:2019.
(b) The supply system shall be TN-S earthing arrangement.
(c) Wiring shall be concealed in rigid PVC conduits of 1.5mm wall thickness.
(d) Wires shall be FR PVC insulated, single core, stranded copper conductor of 450/750 V grade conforming to IS 694:2010.
(e) Lighting circuits shall be wired with 1.5 mm² and power circuits with 4 mm² copper conductors.
(f) Miniature circuit breakers shall conform to IS/IEC 60898-1 and shall have a breaking capacity of 10 kA.
(g) MCB ratings shall range from 6 A to 32 A as shown in the single line diagram.
(h) RCCBs with rated residual operating current of 30 mA shall be provided for all socket outlet circuits and shall conform to IS 12640 (Part 1). Distribution boards and enclosures shall be of suitable size.
(i) Earthing shall be carried out in accordance with IS 3043:2018.
(j) Two numbers of pipe earth electrodes shall be provided for the main distribution board.
(k) The earth resistance shall not exceed 1 ohm.

SECTION 4: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
(a) Wires shall be tested in accordance with IS 10810. On completion, insulation resistance and earth continuity tests shall be carried out in accordance with IS 732.
(b) Wires, MCBs and RCCBs shall be ISI marked. The equipment shall be inspected by the Engineer-in-charge at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.
