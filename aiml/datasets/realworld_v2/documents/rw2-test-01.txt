SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers supply and installation of borewell submersible pumpsets and water quality testing complete in all respects.

3 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — BOREWELL SUBMERSIBLE PUMPSETS
3.1 Submersible pumpsets shall be manufactured to IS 8034:2018.
3.2 Duty point of each pumpset shall be 18 cum/hr at 90 m total head.
3.3 Overall pumpset efficiency at duty point shall be minimum 52 per cent.
3.4 The submersible motor shall be of rewindable wet type as per IS 9283:2013.
3.5 Flat submersible cables of 3 core 6 sq mm copper, PVC insulated, shall comply with IS 694:2010 and be tested to IS 10810.
3.6 The starter panel shall include dry run protection, overload relay and single phasing preventer.

PART D — TREATED WATER QUALITY
4.1 The quality of water supplied shall meet IS 10500:2012.
4.2 pH value: 6.5 to 8.5
4.3 TDS of supplied water shall be within 2000 mg/litre.
4.4 Turbidity of treated water shall be limited to 1 N.T.U..
4.5 Fluoride content shall be within 1.0 mg/litre.
4.6 Nitrate as NO3 shall be less than 45 mg/litre.
4.7 Arsenic content in supplied water shall be less than 0.01 mg/litre.
4.8 Total hardness shall be within 200 mg/litre as CaCO3.
4.9 Total coliforms shall be absent in any 100 ml sample.

5 TESTS AND INSPECTION
5.1 Physico-chemical tests on water samples shall follow IS 3025.
5.2 Pre-dispatch inspection shall be offered to the Department's authorised representative with 15 days notice.

6 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
6.1 Monthly water quality reports shall be furnished to the Department.
6.2 Shop drawings and schematic diagrams shall be got approved from the Engineer-in-charge before manufacture.
6.3 O&M manuals, as-built drawings and catalogues shall be furnished in triplicate at the time of handing over.
6.4 The supplier shall replace free of cost any part found defective within the defect liability period of 24 months.
6.5 Training of two operators for one week shall be imparted by the supplier after commissioning.
