3 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — HORIZONTAL CENTRIFUGAL PUMPS
1. Horizontal centrifugal pumps for clear cold water shall comply with IS 1520:1980.
2. Duty point of each pump shall be 250 m3/hr discharge at 45 mtrs total dynamic head.
3. Pump casing shall be CI and impeller of leaded tin bronze.
4. Sound pressure level shall be limited to 85 dB(A) at 1 mtrs distance.

PUMP DATA SHEET

Total dynamic head: 45 mtrs
Make and type: (to be furnished by the bidder)

4 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — DRIVE MOTORS
1. The drive motor shall be a three phase TEFC squirrel cage induction motor as per IS 325:1996.
2. Motor rating shall be 37 KW continuous.
3. Motors shall operate satisfactorily on a supply of 415 V ± 10 per cent and 50 Hz ± 5 per cent. The motor shall be premium efficiency IE3 conforming to IS 12615:2018.
4. Motor enclosure shall be totally enclosed fan cooled with IP 55 protection.
5. Insulation shall be class F and the motor shall be rated for S1 continuous duty.
6. Motor speed shall be 1500 r.p.m. (4 pole).

SECTION 5: LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
1. LT power cables shall be XLPE insulated 1.1 kV grade armoured cables with aluminium conductors as per IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
2. Size of the incoming feeder cable shall be 3.5 core x 300 sq mm.
3. Continuous conductor operating temperature of XLPE cables shall be 90 Deg.C.
4. Copper control cables of 2.5 sq mm shall be PVC insulated heavy duty type as per IS 1554 (Part 1).
5. Cable conductors shall meet the resistance requirements of IS 8130 for class 2 conductors. Cables shall be laid directly in ground at a depth of 750 mm. with brick protection.
6. Terminations shall be made with brass double compression glands and tinned copper lugs. Cable drums shall carry marking of size, type, length and manufacturer's name.

6 TESTS AND INSPECTION
1. Performance tests on each pump shall be conducted as per IS 9137 in the presence of the Engineer.
2. Pumps shall carry the BIS Standard Mark.
3. Each motor shall be subjected to routine tests in accordance with IS 4029.
4. Acceptance tests on cables shall be carried out as per IS 10810 in presence of the Engineer.
5. Manufacturer's test certificates shall accompany every cable drum supplied. Pre-dispatch inspection shall be offered to the Department's authorised representative with 15 days notice.

7 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
1. Shop drawings and schematic diagrams shall be got approved from the Engineer-in-charge before manufacture. O&M manuals, as-built drawings and catalogues shall be furnished in triplicate at the time of handing over.
2. The supplier shall replace free of cost any part found defective within the defect liability period of 24 months.
3. Training of two operators for one week shall be imparted by the supplier after commissioning.
