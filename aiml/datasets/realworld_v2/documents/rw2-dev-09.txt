SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers rural piped water supply scheme — pumping and treated water supply complete in all respects.

SECTION 3: HORIZONTAL CENTRIFUGAL PUMPS
3.1 Pumps shall conform to IS 1520:1980.
3.2 Each pump shall deliver 250 m³/h against a total head of 45 metres.
3.3 The pump efficiency at the duty point shall not be less than 78%. Casing shall be of cast iron and impeller shall be of bronze.
3.4 Noise level at 1 metres from the pumpset shall not exceed 85 dBA.

PUMP DATA SHEET
Discharge: 250 m³/h
Total head: 45 metres
Make and type: (to be furnished by the bidder)

SECTION 4: DRIVE MOTORS
4.1 Motors shall be squirrel cage induction motors conforming to IS 325:1996.
4.2 The rated output of the motor shall be 37kW.
4.3 Motors shall be suitable for 415V ± 10%, 50 Hz ± 5%, 3 phase supply.
4.4 Motors shall be of efficiency class IE3 as per IS 12615:2018.
4.5 Degree of protection of the motor enclosure shall be IP55.
4.6 Motors shall have class F insulation with temperature rise limited to class B and shall be suitable for S1 duty.
4.7 Synchronous speed of the motor shall be 1500 RPM.

PART E — TREATED WATER QUALITY
5.1 Treated water supplied shall conform to IS 10500:2012.
5.2 PH OF TREATED WATER SHALL BE BETWEEN 6.5 AND 8.5.
5.3 TOTAL DISSOLVED SOLIDS SHALL NOT EXCEED 500 MG/L. Turbidity shall not be more than 1 NTU.
5.4 Fluoride shall not exceed 1.0 mg/L.
5.5 Nitrate shall not exceed 45 mg/L.
5.6 Arsenic shall not exceed 0.01 mg/L.
5.7 Total hardness as CaCO3 shall not exceed 200 mg/L.
5.8 E. coli shall not be detectable in any 100 ml sample.

6 TESTS AND INSPECTION
6.1 Pumps shall be tested at the works in accordance with IS 9137.
6.2 Pumps shall bear the ISI mark.
6.3 Routine tests on motors shall be carried out as per IS 4029.
6.4 Motors shall bear the ISI mark. Water samples shall be tested in a NABL accredited laboratory as per IS 3025 and IS 1622.
6.5 The equipment shall be inspected by the Engineer-in-charge at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.

7 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
7.1 Test reports shall be submitted to the Engineer-in-charge for each source.
7.2 The contractor shall submit detailed shop drawings for approval within 21 days of award of work.
7.3 THREE SETS OF OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE MANUALS SHALL BE HANDED OVER ON COMPLETION.
7.4 THE COMPLETE INSTALLATION SHALL BE GUARANTEED AGAINST DEFECTIVE DESIGN, MATERIAL AND WORKMANSHIP FOR A PERIOD OF 12 MONTHS FROM THE DATE OF HANDING OVER.
7.5 The contractor shall train the Department's operating staff in operation and routine maintenance.
