KARNATAKA PUBLIC WORKS, PORTS AND INLAND WATER TRANSPORT DEPARTMENT
Electrical Sub-Division, Dharwad
Short Tender Notification No. EE/PWD/DWD/ELE/09/2026-27
Name of work: Internal electrification of the 120-bed girls' hostel block at Government Polytechnic, Dharwad.
Estimated cost: Rs. 38.4 lakhs
Class of contractor: Class I electrical contractor licensed by the Government of Karnataka

GENERAL
1. The contractor shall possess a valid electrical contractor licence and shall employ a licensed supervisor on the work.
2. The work shall be carried out as per the Karnataka PWD Schedule of Rates 2025-26 and IS 732:1989.
3. The contractor shall obtain approval of the Electrical Inspectorate before energisation.

WIRING
4. Wiring shall be concealed in rigid PVC conduits of 1.5 mm wall thickness.
5. Wires shall be 1100 V grade, FR PVC insulated, single core, stranded copper conductor, conforming to IS 694.
6. Lighting circuits shall be wired with 1.5 sq mm and power circuits with 4 sq mm conductors.
7. Separate protective earth conductor of the same size as the phase conductor shall be run along with each circuit.
8. Wires shall be ISI marked and of approved make.
9. Switches and sockets shall be of modular type, 6 A and 16 A as required.

DISTRIBUTION BOARDS AND PROTECTION
10. Distribution boards shall be double door, powder coated, of suitable size.
11. MCBs shall conform to IS 8828 and shall have a breaking capacity of 10 kA.
12. RCCBs with 30 mA sensitivity shall be provided on each floor distribution board.
13. The main incomer to the building shall be a 100 A, 4 pole MCCB with 25 kA breaking capacity.

EARTHING
14. Earthing shall be done as per IS 3043.
15. Two pipe earth electrodes shall be provided for the building distribution board.
16. The earth resistance shall not exceed 2 ohm.

TESTING AND COMPLETION
17. On completion, the installation shall be tested and the test results recorded in the prescribed form.
18. Insulation resistance of the installation shall not be less than 1 MΩ.
19. Completion drawings showing the wiring layout shall be submitted in triplicate.
20. The defects liability period shall be 12 months from the date of completion.

Note: Specification items not covered above shall be as per the relevant IS and the directions of the Engineer-in-charge.
