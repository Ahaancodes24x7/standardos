SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers supply and installation of borewell submersible pumpsets and water quality testing complete in all respects.

3 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — BOREWELL SUBMERSIBLE PUMPSETS
(a) Borewell submersible pumpsets shall conform to IS 8034:2018.
(b) Each pumpset shall deliver 18 m3/hr against a total head of 90 m.
(c) Motors shall be water filled, wet type, conforming to IS 9283:2013.
(d) Submersible flat cables shall be 3 core, 6 mm², copper conductor, PVC insulated, conforming to IS 694:2010 and tested as per IS 10810. Each pumpset shall be provided with an automatic starter with single phasing preventer and dry run protection.

PART D — TREATED WATER QUALITY
(a) Treated water supplied shall conform to IS 10500:2012.
(b) pH of treated water shall be between 6.5 and 8.5.
(c) Total dissolved solids shall not exceed 500 mg/l.
(d) Turbidity shall not be more than 5 NTU.
(e) Fluoride shall not exceed 1.0 mg/l.
(f) Nitrate shall not exceed 45 mg/l. Arsenic shall not exceed 0.01 mg/l.
(g) Total hardness as CaCO3 shall not exceed 200 mg/l.
(h) E. coli shall not be detectable in any 100 ml sample.

5 TESTS AND INSPECTION
(a) Pumpsets shall bear the BIS Standard Mark.
(b) Water samples shall be tested in a NABL accredited laboratory as per IS 3025 and IS 1622.
(c) The equipment shall be inspected by the Engineer-in-charge at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.

6 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
(a) Test reports shall be submitted to the Engineer-in-charge for each source. The contractor shall submit detailed shop drawings for approval within 21 days of award of work.
(b) Three sets of operation and maintenance manuals shall be handed over on completion.
(c) The complete installation shall be guaranteed against defective design, material and workmanship for a period of 12 months from the date of handing over.
(d) The contractor shall train the Department's operating staff in operation and routine maintenance.
