SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers construction of rcc elevated service reservoir complete in all respects.

SITE CONDITIONS
(a) Exposure condition for the design of the structure shall be taken as severe in terms of IS 456:2000.

4 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — CONCRETE AND REINFORCEMENT
(a) Plain and reinforced cement concrete works shall comply with IS 456:2000.
(b) Grade of concrete for all RCC members shall be M30.
(c) The nominal cover to reinforcement shall be 45 millimetre. Maximum free water-cement ratio shall be 0.55.
(d) Cement content shall be not less than 320 kg/cu.m.
(e) OPC 43 grade cement complying with IS 8112 shall be used.
(f) Fe 500D TMT reinforcement bars complying with IS 1786:2008 shall be used.
(g) Mix proportioning of concrete shall be done as per IS 10262:2019.
(h) Concrete shall be cured for not less than 10 days.
(i) Mechanical needle vibrators shall be used for compaction of concrete.

5 TESTS AND INSPECTION
(a) Testing of concrete for compressive strength shall be done as per IS 516:1959.
(b) The contractor shall furnish manufacturer's test certificates for every lot of cement and reinforcement.
(c) Before despatch, the Engineer-in-charge will inspect the equipment at the maker's works.

6 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
(a) Detailed shop drawings are to be submitted by the contractor for approval not later than 21 calendar days after the award of work.
(b) On completion the contractor shall hand over three copies of the O&M manuals.
(c) The contractor shall guarantee the whole installation for 12 months after handing over against faulty design, materials and workmanship.
(d) Operation and routine maintenance training shall be given by the contractor to the Department's operators.
