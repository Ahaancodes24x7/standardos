SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers procurement of distribution transformers and lt cables complete in all respects.

PART C — DISTRIBUTION TRANSFORMERS
- Distribution transformers shall comply with IS 1180 (Part 1):2014. Transformer rating shall be 630 kva, 11/0.433 kV, vector group Dyn11.
- Transformers shall comply with energy efficiency level 2 of IS 1180 (Part 1).
- Transformers shall be outdoor type, copper wound, oil immersed with ONAN cooling.
- The top oil temperature rise shall be limited to 35 ℃ above an ambient of 50 ℃.
- The tank shall be fabricated from welded mild steel with epoxy paint finish.

SECTION 4: LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
- LT power cables shall be aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated, armoured, PVC sheathed, 1.1 kV grade, complying with IS 7098 (Part 1):1988. The size of the main feeder cable shall be 3.5 core 300 mm2.
- Maximum continuous conductor temperature of the cables shall be 90 ℃.
- Copper conductor control cables of 2.5 mm2, PVC insulated, 1.1 kV grade shall comply with IS 1554 (Part 1).
- Class 2 stranded conductors complying with IS 8130 shall be used.
- Cables shall be run in RCC trenches or on perforated trays as per drawings.
- Double compression glands and crimped lugs shall be used for all cable terminations.
- The maker's name, size, voltage grade and year of manufacture shall be marked on every drum.

5 TESTS AND INSPECTION
- Each transformer shall be subjected to all routine tests as per IS 2026 (Part 1):2011.
- Testing of the cables shall be done as per IS 10810.
- Each consignment of cables shall be accompanied by routine test certificates. Before despatch, the Engineer-in-charge will inspect the equipment at the maker's works.

6 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
- Detailed shop drawings are to be submitted by the contractor for approval not later than 21 calendar days after the award of work.
- On completion the contractor shall hand over three copies of the O&M manuals. The contractor shall guarantee the whole installation for 12 months after handing over against faulty design, materials and workmanship.
- Operation and routine maintenance training shall be given by the contractor to the Department's operators.
