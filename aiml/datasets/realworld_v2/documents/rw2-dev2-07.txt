SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers internal electrification including wiring, distribution boards and earthing complete in all respects.

3 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — INTERNAL WIRING AND EARTHING
(a) All wiring work shall comply with IS 732:2019.
(b) The earthing arrangement of the supply system is TN-S.
(c) All wiring shall run concealed in rigid PVC conduit having 1.5 m.m. wall thickness.
(d) Wires are to be single core, stranded copper conductor, FR PVC insulated, 450/750 V grade to IS 694:2010.
(e) Copper conductors of 1.5 mm2 shall be used for lighting circuits and 4 mm2 for power circuits.
(f) MCBs shall comply with IS/IEC 60898-1 and have a rated short-circuit capacity of 10 kA rms.
(g) MCBs from 6 ampere up to 32 ampere shall be provided as per the single line diagram.
(h) All socket outlet circuits shall be protected by 30 mA RCCBs complying with IS 12640 (Part 1).
(i) The earthing installation shall comply with IS 3043:1987.
(j) The main distribution board shall be earthed through two pipe earth electrodes.
(k) Resistance of the earthing system shall be not more than 1 Ohms.

4 TESTS AND INSPECTION
(a) Testing of wires shall be done as per IS 10810.
(b) After completion, the insulation resistance and earth continuity of the installation shall be tested as per IS 732.
(c) All wires, MCBs and RCCBs supplied shall carry the ISI mark.
(d) Before despatch, the Engineer-in-charge will inspect the equipment at the maker's works.

5 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
(a) Detailed shop drawings are to be submitted by the contractor for approval not later than 21 calendar days after the award of work.
(b) On completion the contractor shall hand over three copies of the O&M manuals.
(c) The contractor shall guarantee the whole installation for 12 months after handing over against faulty design, materials and workmanship.
(d) Operation and routine maintenance training shall be given by the contractor to the Department's operators.
