PART B — TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

B1. SUBMERSIBLE PUMPSETS
B1.1 Borewell submersible pumpsets shall conform to IS 8034:2018 and shall bear the BIS Standard Mark.
B1.2 The pumpsets shall be suitable for 150 mm (6 inch) borewells.
B1.3 Each pumpset shall deliver 18 m3/h against a total head of 90 m.
B1.4 The overall efficiency of the pumpset at the duty point shall be not less than 52 %.
B1.5 Motors shall be water filled, wet type, conforming to IS 9283:2013.
B1.6 The motor shall be suitable for operation on 415 V ± 10 %, 50 Hz, 3 phase supply.
B1.7 Rated output of the motor shall be 11 kW.
B1.8 Impellers and diffusers shall be of stainless steel.
B1.9 A non-return valve shall be provided at the delivery of each pumpset.

TECHNICAL DATA SHEET — SUBMERSIBLE PUMPSET
Discharge at duty point: 5 lps
Total head at duty point: 90 m
Motor rating: 11 kW
Speed: 2900 rpm
Borewell size: 150 mm

B2. SUBMERSIBLE CABLES
B2.1 Submersible flat cables shall be 3 core, 10 sq mm, copper conductor, PVC insulated and PVC sheathed, conforming to IS 694:2010.
B2.2 Cables shall be tested in accordance with IS 10810.
B2.3 Cable joints shall be made watertight using vulcanising tape and shall be tested before lowering the pumpset.

B3. STARTER AND CONTROL PANEL
B3.1 Each pumpset shall be provided with a fully automatic star-delta starter housed in a sheet steel enclosure with IP55 degree of protection.
B3.2 The starter shall have single phasing preventer, dry run protection and overload relay.
B3.3 The panel shall be suitable for outdoor mounting on a concrete plinth.
B3.4 Earthing of the panel and pumpset shall be done with two separate earth pits.

B4. WATER QUALITY
B4.1 Water supplied through the scheme shall conform to IS 10500:2012.
B4.2 Total dissolved solids of the supplied water shall not exceed 500 mg/l.
B4.3 Fluoride in the supplied water shall not exceed 1.5 mg/l.
B4.4 Nitrate shall not exceed 45 mg/l.
B4.5 Arsenic content shall not exceed 0.05 mg/l.
B4.6 pH of water shall be in the range of 6.5 to 8.5.
B4.7 Water samples shall be collected before commissioning and tested in a NABL accredited laboratory as per IS 3025.
B4.8 Test reports shall be submitted to the Engineer-in-charge for each source.

B5. INSPECTION AND WARRANTY
B5.1 Pumpsets shall be inspected by the Department's authorised representative at the manufacturer's works.
B5.2 The contractor shall operate and maintain the scheme for a period of 12 months after commissioning.
B5.3 Pumpsets and starters shall carry a warranty of 24 months from the date of commissioning.
