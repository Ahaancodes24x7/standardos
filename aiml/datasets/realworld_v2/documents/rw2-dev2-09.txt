SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers supply and installation of borewell submersible pumpsets and water quality testing complete in all respects.

3 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — BOREWELL SUBMERSIBLE PUMPSETS
(a) SUBMERSIBLE PUMPSETS FOR BOREWELLS SHALL COMPLY WITH IS 8034:2018.
(b) Duty point of each pumpset shall be 18 cu.m/hr at 90 metre total head.
(c) The motors shall be wet type, water filled, complying with IS 9283:2013.
(d) Flat submersible cables shall be copper conductor, PVC insulated, 3 core 6 Sq mm, complying with IS 694:2010 and tested to IS 10810.
(e) An automatic starter with dry run protection and single phasing preventer shall be supplied with every pumpset.

SECTION 4: TREATED WATER QUALITY
(a) The quality of treated water shall comply with IS 10500:2012.
(b) The pH value of treated water shall lie within 6.5 to 8.5.
(c) TDS of treated water shall be limited to 2000 mg per litre.
(d) The turbidity of treated water shall be limited to 1 NTU..
(e) Fluoride (as F) shall be limited to 1.5 mg per litre.
(f) Nitrate (as NO3) shall be limited to 45 mg per litre.
(g) Arsenic (as As) shall be limited to 0.01 mg per litre.
(h) Total hardness (as CaCO3) shall be limited to 200 mg per litre. No E. coli shall be detected in any 100 ml sample.

SECTION 5: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
(a) All pumpsets shall carry the BIS Standard Mark.
(b) Testing of water samples shall be done at a NABL accredited laboratory as per IS 3025.
(c) Before despatch, the Engineer-in-charge will inspect the equipment at the maker's works.
