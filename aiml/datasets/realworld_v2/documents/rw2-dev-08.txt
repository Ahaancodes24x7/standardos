SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers electrical works for sewage treatment plant including motors and panels complete in all respects.

SITE CONDITIONS
- The equipment shall be designed for a maximum ambient temperature of 40 deg C.
- Relative humidity at site may reach 95%.
- The installation is at an altitude of less than 1000 m above mean sea level.

SECTION 4: MAIN LT PANEL
- The LT panel shall conform to IS/IEC 61439-1:2020 and IS/IEC 61439-2:2020.
- The panel shall be cubicle type, floor mounted, fabricated from CRCA sheet steel of 2mm thickness.
- The rated operational voltage shall be 415V, 3 phase, 4 wire, 50Hz.
- Main busbars shall be of electrolytic grade aluminium rated for 1600A continuous current. The busbars and panel shall withstand a short circuit current of 50 kA for 1 second.
- The degree of protection of the panel enclosure shall be IP54.
- The switchboard shall have Form 3b internal separation.
- Incomers shall be 1600A, 4 pole, electrically operated air circuit breakers conforming to IS/IEC 60947-2:2003.
- Outgoing feeders shall be MCCBs with ultimate breaking capacity of 36 kA.
- Multifunction meters of accuracy class 0.5 shall be provided on each incomer.

5 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — DRIVE MOTORS
- Motors shall be squirrel cage induction motors conforming to IS 325:1996.
- The rated output of the motor shall be 37kW. Motors shall be suitable for 415V ± 10%, 50Hz ± 5%, 3 phase supply.
- Motors shall be of efficiency class IE3 as per IS 12615:2018.
- Degree of protection of the motor enclosure shall be IP55. Motors shall have class F insulation with temperature rise limited to class B and shall be suitable for S1 duty.
- Synchronous speed of the motor shall be 1500 RPM.

PART F — LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
- Power cables shall be 1.1 kV grade, aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated, armoured and PVC sheathed, conforming to IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
- The main feeder cable shall be 3.5 core 300 sq mm.
- The cables shall be suitable for a maximum continuous conductor temperature of 90 deg C.
- Control cables shall be PVC insulated, copper conductor of 2.5 sq mm, 1.1 kV grade, conforming to IS 1554 (Part 1).
- Conductors shall be stranded, class 2, conforming to IS 8130.
- Cables shall be laid in RCC trenches or on perforated cable trays as shown in the drawings.
- Cable ends shall be terminated using double compression glands and crimped lugs. Each drum shall be marked with the manufacturer's name, size, voltage grade and year of manufacture.

SECTION 7: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
- Routine verification of each assembly shall be carried out in accordance with IS/IEC 61439-1.
- Type test certificates of the offered panel design from a NABL accredited laboratory shall be submitted.
- Routine tests on motors shall be carried out as per IS 4029.
- Motors shall bear the ISI mark.
- Cables shall be tested in accordance with IS 10810.
- Routine test certificates of cables shall be furnished with each consignment.
- The equipment shall be inspected by the Engineer-in-charge at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.
