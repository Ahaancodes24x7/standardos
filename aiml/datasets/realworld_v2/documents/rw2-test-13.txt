SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers construction of rcc elevated service reservoir complete in all respects.

SITE CONDITIONS
(a) Exposure condition shall be considered as severe for all members.

PART D — CONCRETE AND REINFORCEMENT
(a) Design and construction of RCC shall be in accordance with IS 456:2000.
(b) Grade of concrete for all structural members shall be M 20.
(c) Clear cover to main reinforcement shall be 30mm for all members.
(d) Maximum free water cement ratio shall be 0.45.
(e) Cement content shall not be less than 320 kg/m³ of concrete.
(f) OPC 43 grade cement as per IS 269:2015 shall be used.
(g) Aggregates shall comply with the requirements of IS 383:2016.
(h) High strength deformed bars of grade Fe 500D as per IS 1786:1985 shall be used.
(i) Mix design shall be carried out in accordance with IS 10262:2019.
(j) Concrete shall be kept continuously moist for at least 10 days.
(k) Compaction shall be done by immersion vibrators.

SECTION 5: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
(a) Cube tests for compressive strength shall be done in accordance with IS 516:1959.
(b) Mill test certificates for steel and test certificates for cement shall be submitted for every consignment.
(c) Pre-dispatch inspection shall be offered to the Department's authorised representative with 15 days notice.

6 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
(a) Shop drawings and schematic diagrams shall be got approved from the Engineer-in-charge before manufacture.
(b) O&M manuals, as-built drawings and catalogues shall be furnished in triplicate at the time of handing over.
(c) The supplier shall replace free of cost any part found defective within the defect liability period of 24 months.
(d) Training of two operators for one week shall be imparted by the supplier after commissioning.
