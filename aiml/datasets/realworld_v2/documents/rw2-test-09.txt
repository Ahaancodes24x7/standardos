SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers internal electrification including wiring, distribution boards and earthing complete in all respects.

PART C — INTERNAL WIRING AND EARTHING
(a) The internal wiring installation shall comply with IS 732:2019.
(b) Earthing arrangement of the installation shall be TN-S system.
(c) All wiring shall be drawn through heavy gauge PVC conduits recessed in walls. All enclosures shall be of adequate size to accommodate the equipment.
(d) Building wires shall be 1100 V grade PVC insulated multistrand copper conductor as per IS 694:2010.
(e) Minimum conductor size shall be 1.5 Sq.mm for lighting points and 4 Sq.mm for power points.
(f) MCBs shall be C-curve, as per IS/IEC 60898-1, with rated short circuit capacity of 10 kA.
(g) Final circuit MCBs shall be rated between 6 Amps and 32 Amps.
(h) Socket outlet circuits shall be protected by 30 mA RCCBs conforming to IS 12640 (Part 1). The earthing system shall be designed and installed as per IS 3043:2018.
(i) Main distribution board shall be earthed through two separate pipe earth electrodes.
(j) Combined resistance of the earthing system shall be less than 1 Ohm.
