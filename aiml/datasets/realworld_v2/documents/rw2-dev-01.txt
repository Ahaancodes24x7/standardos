SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers supply, installation, testing and commissioning of lt electrical distribution system complete in all respects.

SITE CONDITIONS
(a) The equipment shall be designed for a maximum ambient temperature of 40°C.
(b) Relative humidity at site may reach 95 %.
(c) The installation is at an altitude of 2600 m above mean sea level.

PART D — MAIN LT PANEL
(a) The LT panel shall conform to IS/IEC 61439-1:2020 and IS/IEC 61439-2:2020.
(b) The panel shall be cubicle type, floor mounted, fabricated from CRCA sheet steel of 2mm thickness.
(c) The rated operational voltage shall be 415 V, 3 phase, 4 wire, 50 Hz.
(d) Main busbars shall be of electrolytic grade aluminium rated for 1600A continuous current.
(e) The busbars and panel shall withstand a short circuit current of 50 kA for 1 second.
(f) The degree of protection of the panel enclosure shall be IP54.
(g) The switchboard shall have Form 3b internal separation.
(h) Incomers shall be 1600A, 4 pole, electrically operated air circuit breakers conforming to IS/IEC 60947-2:2003.
(i) Outgoing feeders shall be MCCBs with ultimate breaking capacity of 36 kA.
(j) Multifunction meters of accuracy class 0.5 shall be provided on each incomer.

PART E — LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
(a) Power cables shall be 1.1 kV grade, aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated, armoured and PVC sheathed, conforming to IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
(b) The cables shall be suitable for a maximum continuous conductor temperature of 90°C.
(c) Control cables shall be PVC insulated, copper conductor of 2.5 sq mm, 1.1 kV grade, conforming to IS 1554 (Part 1).
(d) Conductors shall be stranded, class 2, conforming to IS 8130.
(e) Cables shall be laid in RCC trenches or on perforated cable trays as shown in the drawings.
(f) Cable ends shall be terminated using double compression glands and crimped lugs.
(g) Each drum shall be marked with the manufacturer's name, size, voltage grade and year of manufacture.

6 TESTS AND INSPECTION
(a) Routine verification of each assembly shall be carried out in accordance with IS/IEC 61439-1.
(b) Routine test certificates of cables shall be furnished with each consignment.
(c) The equipment shall be inspected by the Engineer-in-charge at the manufacturer's works before dispatch.
