3 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — HORIZONTAL CENTRIFUGAL PUMPS
(a) The pumps shall comply with IS 1520:1980.
(b) Duty point of each pump shall be 250 m^3/h at 45 metre total head.
(c) Pump efficiency at the duty point shall be 78% (percent) minimum.
(d) The pump casing shall be cast iron and the impeller bronze.
(e) The pumpset noise level measured 1 metre away shall be limited to 85 decibels (A).

PUMP DATA SHEET
Rated discharge: 250 m^3/h
Rated total head: 45 metre
Make and type: (to be furnished by the bidder)

4 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — DRIVE MOTORS
(a) Squirrel cage induction motors complying with IS 325:1996 shall be supplied.
(b) Motor rated output shall be 37 kilowatt.
(c) Motors are to operate on 415 V AC ± 10% (percent), 50 Hertz ± 5% (percent), three phase supply.
(d) Motors shall meet efficiency class IE3 of IS 12615:2018.
(e) The motor enclosure shall have IP55 degree of protection.
(f) Insulation shall be class F, with temperature rise restricted to class B, for continuous S1 duty.
(g) The motor synchronous speed shall be 1500 rev/min.

PART E — LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
(a) LT power cables shall be aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated, armoured, PVC sheathed, 1.1 kV grade, complying with IS 7098 (Part 1):1988.
(b) The size of the main feeder cable shall be 3.5 core 300 Sq mm.
(c) Maximum continuous conductor temperature of the cables shall be 90 ℃.
(d) Copper conductor control cables of 2.5 Sq mm, PVC insulated, 1.1 kV grade shall comply with IS 1554 (Part 1).
(e) Class 2 stranded conductors complying with IS 8130 shall be used.
(f) Cables shall be run in RCC trenches or on perforated trays as per drawings.
(g) Double compression glands and crimped lugs shall be used for all cable terminations.
(h) The maker's name, size, voltage grade and year of manufacture shall be marked on every drum.

SECTION 6: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
(a) Works testing of the pumps shall be done as per IS 9137.
(b) Each motor shall be subjected to routine tests as per IS 4029.
(c) All motors shall carry the ISI mark.
(d) Testing of the cables shall be done as per IS 10810.
(e) Each consignment of cables shall be accompanied by routine test certificates.
(f) Before despatch, the Engineer-in-charge will inspect the equipment at the maker's works.

7 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
(a) Detailed shop drawings are to be submitted by the contractor for approval not later than 21 calendar days after the award of work.
(b) On completion the contractor shall hand over three copies of the O&M manuals.
(c) The contractor shall guarantee the whole installation for 12 months after handing over against faulty design, materials and workmanship.
(d) Operation and routine maintenance training shall be given by the contractor to the Department's operators.
