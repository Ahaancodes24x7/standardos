SCOPE OF WORK
This specification covers electrical works for sewage treatment plant including motors and panels complete in all respects.

SITE CONDITIONS
- All equipment shall be suitable for operation at an ambient air temperature not exceeding 40 °C.
- The equipment shall be suitable for relative humidity up to 95 per cent.
- Site altitude does not exceed 1000 M above MSL.

SECTION 4: MAIN LT PANEL
- Switchboards shall be designed, manufactured and tested in accordance with IS/IEC 61439-1:2020 and IS/IEC 61439-2:2020. Panels shall be free standing, totally enclosed, made of 2 mm. thick CRCA sheet with powder coated finish.
- The panel shall be suitable for 415 volts ± 10 per cent, 50 c/s, 3 phase 4 wire system.
- The horizontal busbar shall be high conductivity aluminium alloy of 1600 A rating.
- Short time withstand rating of the switchboard shall be 50 KA for 1 s.
- Enclosure protection class for the switchboard shall be IP 54.
- Internal separation of functional units shall be Form 4.
- Incoming breaker shall be 1600 A four pole draw-out ACB as per IS/IEC 60947-2:2003 with microprocessor release. All outgoing MCCBs shall have a rated service breaking capacity of not less than 36 KA.
- Each incomer shall be provided with a digital multifunction meter with RS-485 port.

SECTION 5: DRIVE MOTORS
- The drive motor shall be a three phase TEFC squirrel cage induction motor as per IS 325:1996.
- Motor rating shall be 37 KW continuous.
- Motors shall operate satisfactorily on a supply of 415 volts ± 10 per cent and 50 c/s ± 5 per cent.
- The motor shall be of high efficiency design. Motor enclosure shall be totally enclosed fan cooled with IP 55 protection.
- Insulation shall be class F and the motor shall be rated for S1 continuous duty.
- Motor speed shall be 1500 RPM (4 pole).

PART F — LT POWER AND CONTROL CABLES
- LT power cables shall be XLPE insulated 1.1 kV grade armoured cables with aluminium conductors as per IS 7098 (Part 1):1988. Size of the incoming feeder cable shall be 3.5 core x 300 sqmm.
- Continuous conductor operating temperature of XLPE cables shall be 90 °C.
- Copper control cables of 2.5 sqmm shall be PVC insulated heavy duty type as per IS 1554 (Part 1).
- Cable conductors shall meet the resistance requirements of IS 8130 for class 2 conductors.
- Cables shall be laid directly in ground at a depth of 750 mm. with brick protection.
- Terminations shall be made with brass double compression glands and tinned copper lugs.
- Cable drums shall carry marking of size, type, length and manufacturer's name.

SECTION 7: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
- Each switchboard shall be subjected to routine verification tests as per IS/IEC 61439-1 at the works.
- Each motor shall be subjected to routine tests in accordance with IS 4029.
- Manufacturer's test certificates shall accompany every cable drum supplied.
- Pre-dispatch inspection shall be offered to the Department's authorised representative with 15 days notice.
