This specification covers rural piped water supply scheme — pumping and treated water supply complete in all respects.

SECTION 3: HORIZONTAL CENTRIFUGAL PUMPS
1. The pumps shall comply with IS 1520:1980.
2. Discharge of each pump shall be 250 m3/hour.
3. Pump efficiency at the duty point shall be 78% (percent) minimum. The pump casing shall be cast iron and the impeller bronze.
4. The pumpset noise level measured 1 mtr away shall be limited to 85 decibels (A).

PUMP DATA SHEET
Rated discharge: 250 m3/hour
Rated total head: 45 mtr
Make and type: (to be furnished by the bidder)

SECTION 4: DRIVE MOTORS
1. Squirrel cage induction motors complying with IS 325:1996 shall be supplied.
2. Motor rated output shall be 37 k.W.. Motors are to operate on 415-volt ± 10% (percent), 50 Hertz ± 5% (percent), three phase supply.
3. Motors shall meet efficiency class IE3 of IS 12615:2018.
4. Insulation shall be class F, with temperature rise restricted to class B, for continuous S1 duty.
5. The motor synchronous speed shall be 1500 rpm..

PART E — TREATED WATER QUALITY
1. The quality of treated water shall comply with IS 10500:2012.
2. The pH value of treated water shall lie within 6.5 to 8.5.
3. TDS of treated water shall be limited to 500 mg per litre.
4. The turbidity of treated water shall be limited to 5 NTU..
5. Fluoride (as F) shall be limited to 1.0 mg per litre.
6. Nitrate (as NO3) shall be limited to 45 mg per litre.
7. Arsenic (as As) shall be limited to 0.01 mg per litre.
8. Total hardness (as CaCO3) shall be limited to 200 mg per litre.
9. No E. coli shall be detected in any 100 ml sample.

SECTION 6: TESTING, INSPECTION AND CERTIFICATION
1. Works testing of the pumps shall be done as per IS 9137.
2. Each motor shall be subjected to routine tests as per IS 4029.
3. All motors shall carry the ISI mark.
4. Testing of water samples shall be done at a NABL accredited laboratory as per IS 3025 and IS 1622.
5. Before despatch, the Engineer-in-charge will inspect the equipment at the maker's works.

7 DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTY AND TRAINING
1. The contractor shall submit test reports for every source to the Engineer-in-charge.
2. Detailed shop drawings are to be submitted by the contractor for approval not later than 21 calendar days after the award of work.
3. On completion the contractor shall hand over three copies of the O&M manuals.
4. The contractor shall guarantee the whole installation for 12 months after handing over against faulty design, materials and workmanship.
5. Operation and routine maintenance training shall be given by the contractor to the Department's operators.
